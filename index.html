📖 Kasi Casino Rules

The 40-Card Deck

In South African Kasi Casino, all face cards (Jacks, Queens, Kings) are removed. Only Ace through 10 are used (40 cards total). Ace is strictly 1.

3-Hand Deal (10-10-10-10)

Each of the 3 players gets 10 cards, and 10 cards are dealt face-up on the table layout. The entire 40-card deck is in play!

Core Turn Actions

  • Pair Capture: Match cards of the exact same rank.
  • Sum Capture: Capture cards whose values add up to your played card (e.g., 9 captures 5+4 and 7+2 at once).
  • Building: Combine a hand card with table cards into a target rank. Rule: You MUST hold a card of the target rank in hand!
  • Locked Double Builds: When two sets of the same rank are merged, the build is locked! Nobody can increase it; only capturing it with that rank is allowed.
  • Increasing Builds: An opponent can steal an unlocked single build by adding a card to raise the total, if they hold the higher target rank.
  • Trailing: Placing a card face-up. Rule: You cannot trail if you own an active build!

Scoring (11 Base Points + Sweeps)

  • Most Cards (3 pts): Highest card count (out of 40). 0 pts if tied.
  • Most Spades (1 pt): Highest Spades count (out of 10). 0 pts if tied.
  • Big Casino (10♦): 2 pts to capturer.
  • Little Casino (2♠): 1 pt to capturer.
  • Aces: 1 pt each (4 pts total).
  • Sweeps: 1 bonus pt whenever you clear the entire table in one move!

Last Capture & Count Out

Whoever makes the last capture of the round takes all leftover cards on the table. First to 21 points wins the match! You can declare Count Out mid-round if you reach 21, but miscounting results in an automatic loss!
